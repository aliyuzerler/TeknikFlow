# Product Principles
- Design for dispatchers and field technicians first; reduce coordination overhead.
- The work order is the central operational record, linked to customer, site, asset, team, parts, labor, evidence, signature and report.
- Minimize double entry; preserve an audit trail.
- Make operational state visible. Never fake sync, GPS, payment or signature success.
- Configure workflows only where needed; avoid a generic low-code platform in the MVP.
