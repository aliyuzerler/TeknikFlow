# TeknikFlow Brand Notes

**Name:** TeknikFlow  
**Descriptor:** Saha Servis ve Operasyon Yönetimi  
**Tagline:** Servis operasyonunuz, ofisten sahaya tek akışta.  
**Alternative tagline:** Her iş emri kontrol altında.

## Wordmark
Use a clean geometric sans-serif wordmark. “Teknik” regular/medium and “Flow” semibold may be differentiated subtly. Symbol: two parallel route segments joining into a forward-moving T path. Avoid literal gears, wrench clip-art and generic lightning bolts.

## Brand voice
Clear, reliable, competent and action-oriented. Prefer “Atandı”, “Yola çıktı”, “Sahada”, “Parça bekliyor”, “Müşteri onayı bekliyor”, “Tamamlandı” over vague labels. Turkish is default; avoid unnecessary English jargon in customer-facing UI.

## Naming disclaimer
This is a preliminary product naming choice only. Search-engine checks are not legal trademark clearance and do not establish domain or social handle availability. Verify relevant Nice classes, Turkish trademark registry, WIPO where relevant, domains and company name before public launch.
